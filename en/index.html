How to use merkio

🌐

Web app (in the browser)

Ready to use right away on phone, tablet and PC – no installation. Works on iPhone and iPad too.

  1. Open app.merkio.de in your browser.
  2. Create an account, then create a group or join with a code.
  3. Optionally choose “Add to Home Screen” in the browser menu – merkio then launches like a real app.

📲 Install as an app on your home screen

iPhone/iPad: Open app.merkio.de in Safari → tap the share icon ⎋ (square with an arrow pointing up) → choose “Add to Home Screen” → “Add”. merkio then appears with its own icon like a regular app.

Android (browser): In Chrome open the menu ⋮ → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Open web app
🤖

Android app

Native app as a direct download – with push notifications.

  1. Tap “Android app (APK)” – the file merkio.apk will be saved.
  2. When opening it, allow installation from unknown sources if Android asks.
  3. Open the app, create an account, create or join a group – done.

Frequently asked questions

How can I share a shopping list with others in real time?

Create a group in merkio and invite family, flatmates or club members via invite code or link. Every change – such as a checked-off item – appears instantly for all members, with no need to refresh.

Is merkio really free?

Yes. merkio is completely free – no ads, no hidden costs and no subscription.

Do I need to install anything? Does merkio work on iPhone?

No, you can use merkio as a web app right in your browser – on iPhone, iPad, Android and PC, with no installation. There is also a free native Android app.

Who is merkio for?

For families, flat-shares, clubs and teams – or just for yourself. Anywhere lists are shared and tasks are divided up.

Can I manage shopping lists and to-do lists together?

Yes. merkio offers shopping lists, to-do lists with priorities (A/B/C), due dates and assignees, and notes lists – all in one place.

How does the recipe import work?

Paste a recipe text or simply name the dish – merkio detects the ingredients and adds them to your shopping list automatically, scaled to the number of servings you want.
